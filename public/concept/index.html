What is Home Design English?

HDE is a comprehensive home-planning platform offering residential construction cost calculators, 2D architectural concept floor plans, interior renovation estimators, and a zero-brokerage Bangalore property discovery portal.

What is the average house construction cost per sq ft in India in 2026?

In 2026, standard residential home construction in major cities like Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Pune ranges from ₹1,700 to ₹2,400 per sq ft for standard quality, and ₹3,200 to ₹4,500+ per sq ft for luxury finishes.

How accurate are HDE’s construction cost estimates?

HDE’s estimates use empirical building material ratios (IS 456 standards) and current wholesale market indices. While actual quotes vary by soil condition, elevation design, and local labor rates, HDE typically provides estimates within 5–8% of actual turnkey contractor bids.

Can I build directly from an HDE house plan?

HDE house plans are conceptual architectural layout studies designed for standard Indian plot sizes (20x30, 30x40, 30x50, 40x60). They must be detailed into structural working drawings and certified by a registered local structural engineer for municipal sanction.

Can home owners list their property for rent or sale for free on HDE?

Yes! Individual owners and verified landlords can post flats, houses for rent, and plots for free with 0% brokerage. Owners retain complete control to edit details, delete listings, or mark "Deal Done" once a tenant is finalized.

Can I use HDE calculators without paying?

Yes! All live calculation tools and estimates across India, USA, and UAE modes are 100% free to explore. Premium credits are only required when saving projects to your cloud dashboard or exporting formal PDF reports.
