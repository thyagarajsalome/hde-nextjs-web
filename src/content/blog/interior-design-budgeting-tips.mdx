Interior design can easily cost as much as the structural construction if not planned properly. Here is how you can achieve a premium look on a budget.

## Where to SPEND (Do Not Compromise)
1. **Kitchen Hardware:** Invest in high-quality hinges, channels, and hydraulic systems (like Hettich or Blum). Cheap hardware will rust and break within a year.
2. **Bathroom Fittings:** Faucets and concealed flush tanks are difficult to repair once installed. Always buy reputed brands with long warranties.
3. **Lighting:** Good lighting makes cheap furniture look expensive. Invest in a layered lighting plan (ambient, task, and accent lights).

## Where to SAVE (Cut Costs)
1. **False Ceilings:** You do not need complex, multi-layered POP false ceilings in every room. Keep it simple with a peripheral ceiling just to hide the wiring.
2. **Veneer vs. Laminate:** Wood veneers look beautiful but require expensive polishing and maintenance. High-quality textured laminates provide the same look at a fraction of the cost.
3. **Custom Furniture:** Instead of getting everything custom-built by a carpenter, mix custom wardrobes with ready-made loose furniture (sofas, dining tables) bought during seasonal sales.
