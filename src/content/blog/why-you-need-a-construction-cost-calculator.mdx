The traditional way of building a house involves asking a local contractor, *"How much will it cost?"* The contractor replies, *"Around 25 Lakhs."* By the time the house is finished, you have spent 35 Lakhs.

Why does this happen? Because verbal estimates are just guesses. To protect your hard-earned money, you need a **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)**.

## What is a BOQ?
A BOQ is a detailed list specifying the exact quantity of every material needed—from the tons of steel to the number of tiles—along with the current market rate in your city.

## Why a Digital Calculator is Better than a Manual Estimate
1. **Instant Updates:** Material prices change daily. A digital calculator pulls the latest cement and steel rates for your specific city (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, etc.).
2. **Fraud Prevention:** If the calculator says your foundation needs 4 tons of steel, and your contractor asks for 6 tons, you instantly know something is wrong.
3. **Scenario Planning:** You can easily check how your budget changes if you switch from Standard quality materials to Premium quality materials.

Do not start construction blindly. Arm yourself with data.
