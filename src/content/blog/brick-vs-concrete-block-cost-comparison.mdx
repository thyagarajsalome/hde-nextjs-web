Choosing the right walling material is a critical decision. Today, builders are torn between traditional Red Clay Bricks and Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Blocks. 

## Traditional Red Bricks
**Pros:**
- Excellent thermal mass, keeping interiors cooler.
- High compressive strength, ideal for load-bearing structures.
- Easy to chase for plumbing and electrical conduits.

**Cons:**
- Heavy, increasing the dead load on the foundation and requiring more steel.
- High water absorption, leading to dampness issues.

## AAC Blocks
**Pros:**
- Extremely lightweight, which drastically reduces the structural load. This saves you money on foundation steel and concrete.
- Excellent acoustic and thermal insulation.
- Larger size means faster construction and less mortar usage.

**Cons:**
- Prone to surface cracks if the mortar joints aren't cured properly.
- Cannot be used for load-bearing walls (they require an RCC framed structure).

## The Verdict
If you are building an RCC framed multi-story house, **AAC Blocks** are the clear winner due to the massive savings in steel and mortar.
