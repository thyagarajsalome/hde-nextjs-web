When contractors give you an estimate of "₹1,800 per square foot," they almost always leave out crucial expenses. Here are 5 hidden costs you must include in your budget:

## 1. Government Approvals and Fees
Getting your building plan sanctioned by the local municipal corporation (BBMP, BMC, etc.) involves official fees, architect sign-offs, and sometimes unofficial facilitation charges. 

## 2. Temporary Electricity and Water
Before construction begins, you need a temporary electricity meter and a borewell or water tanker supply. Commercial electricity rates apply to construction sites, which are significantly higher than residential rates.

## 3. Compound Wall and Gates
Contractors calculate the built-up area of the house. The boundary wall, heavy iron entry gates, and exterior paving/landscaping are almost always billed separately.

## 4. Anti-Termite and Waterproofing
Basic estimates rarely include comprehensive chemical waterproofing for the roof and bathrooms, or pre-construction anti-termite treatment for the foundation. Skipping these will cost you lakhs in repairs later.

## 5. Material Wastage
Sand blows away, cement hardens if exposed to moisture, and tiles break during transit. Always account for a 5% to 10% wastage margin on all finishing materials.
