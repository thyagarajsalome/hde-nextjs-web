### The Average Cost of a Kitchen Remodel in 2026

The national average for a standard kitchen remodel sits right around **$25,000 to $40,000**. However, this number can fluctuate wildly based on three primary factors:

1. **Size of your Kitchen:** A 100 sq. ft. galley kitchen will naturally cost significantly less than a 300 sq. ft. open-concept kitchen with a sprawling island.
2. **Quality of Materials:** Budget-friendly stock cabinets and laminate countertops will keep costs low, while custom solid-wood cabinetry, quartzite counters, and luxury appliances (like Sub-Zero or Wolf) will push you well into the $80,000+ range.
3. **Layout Changes:** Are you keeping the sink and stove in the exact same place? If so, you save thousands. Moving plumbing lines, gas lines, or knocking down load-bearing walls requires permits and expensive specialized labor.

### Where Does the Money Go?

When you review your estimate, you'll notice a few categories eat up the majority of your budget:

*   **Cabinets and Hardware (30-40%):** This is almost always the largest expense. Semi-custom and custom cabinets are expensive to build and install.
*   **Labor and Installation (20-30%):** Quality contractors are in high demand. Attempting to DIY plumbing or electrical to save on labor is highly discouraged and can void your home insurance.
*   **Appliances and Ventilation (15-20%):** Smart refrigerators, commercial-style ranges, and powerful vent hoods add up fast.
*   **Countertops (10-15%):** Quartz remains the most popular choice for its durability and zero-maintenance appeal, though granite and butcher block are common alternatives.
