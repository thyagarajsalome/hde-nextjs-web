Building a house in India requires careful financial planning. The total cost of construction is generally divided into two main categories: **Civil Work** and **Finishing Work**.

## 1. Civil Work Costs
Civil work involves the core structure of the house. It includes excavation, foundation, RCC (Reinforced Cement Concrete) framing, brickwork, and plastering.
- **Cement & Steel:** These two materials consume the largest chunk of your civil budget. Always use high-grade TMT bars and reputable cement brands for the foundation and roof casting.
- **Bricks/Blocks:** You can choose between red clay bricks, fly ash bricks, or AAC blocks. AAC blocks are lighter and can reduce the overall structural load and cost.

## 2. Finishing Work Costs
Once the skeleton of the house is ready, finishing work begins. This is where costs can vary wildly based on your personal taste.
- **Flooring:** Options range from affordable ceramic tiles to premium Italian marble.
- **Electrical & Plumbing:** Never compromise on the quality of wires and pipes. Concealed wiring and plumbing should be done using ISI-marked materials.
- **Woodwork & Painting:** Custom teak wood doors will cost significantly more than standard flush doors.
