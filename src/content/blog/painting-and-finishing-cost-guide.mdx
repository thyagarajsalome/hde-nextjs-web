Painting is the final touch that brings your house to life. However, estimating the amount of paint required can be tricky because walls require multiple coats of different materials.

## The 3-Step Painting Process
For newly plastered walls, you cannot apply paint directly. The standard process is:
1. **Primer (1 Coat):** Seals the plaster and provides a base.
2. **Wall Putty (2 Coats):** Fills undulations and provides a smooth, glass-like finish.
3. **Paint (2 Coats):** The final color application (Emulsion for interiors, Weather-proof for exteriors).

## How to Calculate Paint Area
The painting area is generally **built-up area × 3.5**. So, if your house is 1,000 sq.ft, you will have roughly 3,500 sq.ft of wall and ceiling area to paint.

## Coverage Rates
- **Putty Coverage:** ~12-15 sq.ft per kg (for 2 coats).
- **Primer Coverage:** ~120-140 sq.ft per liter.
- **Emulsion Paint Coverage:** ~100-120 sq.ft per liter (for 2 coats).

By knowing these coverage rates, you can buy exactly what you need and prevent contractors from over-billing you for materials.
