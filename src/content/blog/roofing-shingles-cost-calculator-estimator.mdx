## How Roofing Materials Are Measured

In the USA, roofing contractors do not quote jobs in square feet. They quote jobs in **"Squares."**
- **1 Square** = 100 square feet of roof area.
- **1 Bundle of Shingles** covers approximately 33.3 square feet.
- **3 Bundles of Shingles** = 1 Square.

If your ground floor area is 2,000 sq ft, your actual roof area is significantly larger due to the pitch (slope) and overhangs. 

## Why Roof Pitch Matters
The steepness of your roof drastically affects the amount of material required. 
- A standard **6/12 pitch** (medium slope) requires multiplying your base footprint by roughly 1.16. 
- A steep **12/12 pitch** (very steep, common in Victorian or A-Frame houses) requires multiplying the footprint by 1.41.

## Don't Forget the Underlayment and Nails
A roof is an entire system, not just shingles. You will also need:
1. **Underlayment:** Synthetic felt paper that protects the wood decking. One standard roll covers about 4 squares (400 sq ft).
2. **Coil Nails:** Generally, contractors use 4-6 nails per shingle. A standard box of 1.25-inch coil nails will cover roughly 20 squares of roofing.
3. **Drip Edge & Flashing:** Metal perimeter trimming to direct water into your gutters and prevent wood rot.
