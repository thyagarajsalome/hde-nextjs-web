A floor plan is the blueprint of your daily life. A poorly designed plan will lead to dark rooms, wasted space, and inflated construction costs. 

## 1. Prioritize Natural Light and Sun Direction
In India, natural ventilation and sunlight are crucial. Ensure your living room and kitchen receive ample morning sunlight (East/North-East orientation), while keeping bedrooms protected from the harsh afternoon sun (South/West).

## 2. Open vs. Closed Layouts
- **Open Layouts:** Combining the living, dining, and kitchen areas makes a small house feel massive. It also saves money by reducing the number of internal partition walls.
- **Closed Layouts:** Better for large joint families where privacy and noise isolation are top priorities.

## 3. Minimize 'Dead Space'
Hallways and long corridors are considered "dead space." You pay to build them, but you can't place furniture in them. Ask your architect to design a plan that eliminates unnecessary corridors.

## 4. Match the Plan to Your Budget
Every corner, curve, and wall adds to your structural cost. A simple rectangular floor plan is the most cost-effective shape to build.
