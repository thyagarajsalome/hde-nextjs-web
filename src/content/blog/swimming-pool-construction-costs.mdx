### Average Pool Construction Costs in 2026

The cost of a swimming pool is entirely dependent on the material you choose and the geographical region where you live.

#### 1. Gunite (Concrete) Pools: $60,000 - $120,000+
Gunite pools are the gold standard for custom pool construction. Because the concrete is sprayed onto a rebar frame, gunite pools can be built in literally any shape or size. They are extremely durable but take the longest to build (often 2 to 3 months) and have a rougher surface that requires regular replastering every 10-15 years.

#### 2. Fiberglass Pools: $45,000 - $85,000
Fiberglass pools are pre-manufactured shells dropped into an excavated hole. They are the fastest to install (often within weeks) and have a smooth, non-porous surface that resists algae, making them incredibly low-maintenance. However, you are limited to the shapes and sizes offered by the manufacturer.

#### 3. Vinyl Liner Pools: $35,000 - $65,000
Vinyl is typically the most affordable in-ground option. While the initial cost is lower, the vinyl liner usually needs to be replaced every 7 to 10 years, which can cost $4,000 to $6,000 each time.

#### 4. Above-Ground Pools: $3,000 - $10,000
If you want to swim on a budget, an above-ground pool is the way to go. They can be installed in a single weekend. The costs increase if you decide to build custom wooden decking around them.

### The Hidden Costs of Pool Construction

When budgeting for a pool, don't forget the extras! The pool shell itself is only part of the project.

*   **Decking and Patios:** Stamped concrete, travertine pavers, or natural stone decking can easily add $10,000 to $30,000 to the project.
*   **Heating:** If you want to swim early in the spring and late into the fall, a gas or electric heat pump is essential (typically $3,000 - $6,000).
*   **Fencing:** Almost all city building codes require a minimum 4-foot fence with self-latching gates around a pool for safety.
*   **Maintenance:** Expect to spend $1,000 to $2,500 annually on chemicals, water, electricity for the pump, and opening/closing services.

### Hiring the Best Pool Contractors

A swimming pool is a massive engineering project involving heavy excavation, structural steel, electrical wiring, and plumbing. Always hire licensed, bonded, and insured pool contractors. Ask to see their portfolio of completed pools, and never pay the full amount upfront—a standard payment schedule is tied to construction milestones (e.g., Excavation, Gunite, Plaster, Completion).
